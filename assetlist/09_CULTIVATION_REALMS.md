# 09 - 12 MAJOR CULTIVATION REALMS

**Tier System:** Mortal → Earthly → Heavenly → Immortal (12 realms total)
Each realm has special visual aura, dao rhythm, breakthrough characteristics

## MORTAL REALM
**Description:** Body awakens. Qi thin and wild. Learning to feel meridians. Environmental absorption slow. Combat is primary driver.

1. **Body Tempering** - Entry mortal. Faint Qi, sweat, muscle, thin wild aura cyan white, body tempering, skin glowing slightly, training dummy, combat focus
2. **Qi Condensation** - Qi becomes visible mist gathering around hands, condensation, thin to dense transition, learning meridians
3. **Foundation Establishment** - Breakthrough pillar of light gold cyan, stable foundation, dantian glowing, pillar connects heaven earth

## EARTHLY REALM
**Description:** Qi dense and purposeful. Visible aura. Environmental absorption accelerates. Dream Cultivation available. Tribulations begin.

4. **Core Formation** - Core glowing in dantian golden sphere, visible aura around body, environmental Qi flowing in faster, core formation
5. **Nascent Soul** - Soul projection small baby soul above head white violet ghostly double, third eye glow, nascent soul
6. **Spirit Severing** - Severing spiritual, cutting karma, sword of spirit, aura sharp, severing gesture

## HEAVENLY REALM
**Description:** Qi becomes a force of nature. Presence affects world (weather, NPCs, animals). Breakthroughs are catastrophic events. Death is real risk.

7. **Void Tribulation** - Tribulation lightning storm, heavenly tribulation, dark clouds lightning striking cultivator, catastrophic breakthrough, death risk, void
8. **Dao Seeking** - Searching for Dao, ancient symbols floating, seeking gesture, Dao symbols gold, confused but determined, seeking path
9. **Dao Merging** - Merging with Dao, becoming one with Dao, body becoming translucent with Dao patterns, gold merging, unity

## IMMORTAL REALM
**Description:** Qi transcends limits. Can fly, reshape terrain, influence city-scale events. Philosophical choices become primary drivers.

10. **True Immortal** - Can fly, floating in air Qi cloud, reshape terrain, city below, white gold immortal aura, transcend limits, flying
11. **Celestial Sovereign** - Sovereign celestial commanding, throne of clouds, celestial armor, commanding presence, city-scale influence, gold celestial
12. **Eternal Dao Ancestor** - Eternal ancestor ultimate, ancient, timeless, starfield background, Dao ancestor, eternal, galaxy aura, ultimate being

- Realm name English only
